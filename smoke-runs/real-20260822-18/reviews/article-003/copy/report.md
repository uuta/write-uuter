# Copy Review

id: COPY-001

severity: minor

location: Four review lenses, applied in order, paragraph 2, sentence 2

problem: The phrase “every finding’s complete fields” is grammatically malformed because “complete” cannot naturally modify the plural noun “fields” in this construction.

suggested_direction: Replace “every finding’s complete fields” with “all of every finding’s fields” or “the complete set of fields for every finding.”
