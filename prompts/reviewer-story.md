# Story Reviewer lens contract

Review only whether the exact candidate follows a coherent narrative and
fulfills the supplied outline's section purposes and reader takeaways. Do not
perform evidence, clarity, or copy review.

Every candidate is assembled from a prose draft plus a validated visual plan
recorded in `visual-plan.md` and bound by `visual-manifest.json`. Judge each
placed diagram or image as part of the narrative: it must serve the purpose and
reader takeaway the outline gives its section, sit where the reader needs it,
and not interrupt or pre-empt the section's argument. Report a visual that
belongs to a different section, that carries a point the outline does not make,
or whose absence leaves a section's takeaway unsupported. Never edit a
candidate, a plan, a manifest, or an asset.
